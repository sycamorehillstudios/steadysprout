Steady Sprout — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Steady Sprout ("the app"), published by Sycamore Hill Studios, is a local-only app.

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All app data stays on your device.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads. The app requires no sign-up and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Purchases (Android). Optional supporter packs are one-time purchases processed entirely by Google Play's billing system. The app never sees or stores your payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Deleting data. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

On iPhone

  • Same promise. Steady Sprout for iPhone collects nothing either: no account, no analytics, no ads and no network features. Your habits and history stay in the app's private storage on your iPhone.
  • Reminders. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone; nothing goes through a server. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.
  • Export. CSV files are written only when you choose, through the iOS Files sheet, to a place you pick.
  • Device backups. Like any app's data, it is included in your own iCloud Backup or computer backup if you have those turned on. Those backups belong to you and follow Apple's terms; we never see them.
  • Purchases. Optional supporter packs are one-time purchases processed entirely by Apple's App Store. The app never sees or stores your payment details; the App Store only tells it which pack your Apple Account owns, so it can unlock that pack's colors and art on your iPhone. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Ratings. If you're ever asked to rate the app, that request is shown by iOS itself.
  • Deleting data. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes everything it stored there.
